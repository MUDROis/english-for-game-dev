How the platform works

1

Lessons open on any device

Progress, exercises and voice recordings stay in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

2

One structure for all 48 lessons

Warm-up → Listening → Reading → Grammar → Grammar → Speaking → Writing → Homework. Each section is 5–15 minutes of the lesson.

3

Speaking is the main section

Grammar is not memorised but spoken: 60-second rounds, picture comparison, speed drills and recording your own voice.

Your progress for lessons 1–12 will appear here once you start marking sections on their pages.
